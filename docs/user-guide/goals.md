# Goals

**Cash & planning → Goals** tracks a target for a metric or KPI over whole months, for example
revenue of 500,000 in 2027 or cash that stays above 100,000.

## Set a goal
Select **New goal** and choose:
- a **metric**: revenue, operating expenses, EBITDA, net profit, cash, or any KPI of the library or
  approved workspace KPIs;
- the **period**, from the first day of a month to the last day of a month (the target date);
- the **target type**:
  - *reach a value*;
  - *change from the baseline* in % (e.g. +15% net profit, −10% operating expenses);
  - *stay at or above*, or *stay at or below*, a threshold throughout (cash);
- the **target**: percentages are entered as percent;
- **at risk within**: how far the likely outcome may miss before the goal is off track (default 5%);
- optionally a **goal-seek driver**, a taxonomy line or section such as `REVENUE` or `COGS`.

The **baseline** is computed when you create the goal: the same months a year earlier, or for cash
the balance on the first day. It never changes. To change the target, select **Change target** and
give a reason; this makes a new version with the same baseline.

## Evaluate
Select **Evaluate** (as of today, or an earlier date). Each evaluation is kept and shows:
- **Actual to date**: up to the last month end on or before that date.
- **Likely outcome**: actuals, then the latest published projection for the remaining months it
  covers (see Projections), otherwise the approved budget. Where neither covers a month, each
  account's average of the last 3 months is used instead. Cash goals use the latest 13-week
  cash forecast. The method is written above the figures, and the outcome is labelled with its
  nature (budget or forecast).
- **Gap**: from the target to the likely outcome, and to the actual.
- **Status**: on track, at risk or off track.
- **What it would take**:
  - the amount and monthly run rate still needed;
  - for ratios, the numerator or denominator needed;
  - the % change of the driver that would reach the target, or "not achievable within ±100%";
  - for cash, the first breach and the additional inflow needed.

  Assumptions are stated: other lines stay as projected, and balances stay at their latest actual
  value.
- **Contribution analysis**: how much each line explains of the difference from the budget (or
  from the same months a year earlier). Any part not explained by single lines is shown as a
  residual.
- **Month by month**: the metric to date, then projected.

Every figure is a stored calculation that can be reproduced and explained down to accounts. If
entries are posted after an evaluation, the page says it is out of date.
