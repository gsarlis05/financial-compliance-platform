# Alerts

**Cash & planning → Alerts** lists what needs attention:
- **Goal status:** a goal became *at risk* (a warning) or *off track* (critical), when it was
  evaluated.
- **Goal threshold:** a goal to stay above or below a threshold is breached, in the actuals or in
  the cash forecast.
- **Budget year to date:** a budget line is unfavourable year to date (costs above budget, income
  below it) by at least the variance thresholds in the budget settings (default €1,000 and 10%).
- **Budget trend:** a budget line was unfavourable in each of the last 3 months.

An alert is raised when something **changes**. While the condition stays the same, it is not
raised again. If a goal goes back on track and later falls behind again, a new alert is raised.

Goal alerts appear with each goal evaluation. To check the budgets, select **Check budgets now**;
the last complete month is checked.

Once you have dealt with an alert, select **Acknowledge**, optionally with a note. The
acknowledgement is shared with everyone who sees the alert and records who made it. Acknowledged
alerts are kept: select **Show acknowledged alerts** to see them.
