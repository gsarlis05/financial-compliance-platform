# My money (personal workspaces)

A personal workspace keeps your own finances, separate from any business workspace. Nothing flows
between them automatically. In a personal workspace the menu shows **My money**: Overview and
Transactions.

## Get started
Select **Set up my accounts**. You get a current account, savings, cash, investments, property, a
business stake, a credit card, a mortgage and other loans, plus income and spending categories.
Add your own accounts or categories at any time.

Enter the **starting balance** of each account on a date. For example: the current account had
2,000 on 1 January, and the mortgage was 150,000.

## Record money in or out
- **Spent:** from an account, to a spending category. For example: 120 from the current account for
  groceries.
- **Received:** into an account, from an income category. For example: salary into the current
  account.
- **Moved between accounts:** for example, paying the credit card from the current account, or
  moving money into savings.

Each one is saved at once. To correct a mistake, select **Undo** and give a reason. The original
stays in the history, marked undone. Underneath, everything is double-entry bookkeeping, so the
figures always add up.

## What something is worth
For a house, investments or a stake in your business, enter what it is worth on a date. This is
shown in your net worth as an estimate (**Valuation**) and is never booked.

## Net worth and spending
- **Overview** shows what you own, what you owe and your net worth today, and at each month end
  over the last year.
- **Transactions** shows a month's income, spending and what is left over, and spending by
  category.

## Budget, goals and the year ahead
**Budget and goals** is in the **My money** menu.

- **Budget:** set a monthly amount for a spending category, from a month onwards. The page shows
  each category's budget against what you spent that month. A new amount replaces the old one from
  its month; earlier months keep the old amount.
- **Goals:**
  - **Savings:** reach an amount in chosen accounts by a date.
  - **Emergency fund:** keep a number of months of your average spending.
  - **Debt payoff:** bring chosen loans or cards to zero by a date.

  Each goal shows where you are, how much you need each month, your pace over the last three
  months and where that pace takes you: **on track**, **at risk** or **off track**. Retire a goal
  you no longer need; it stays in the history.
- **The next 12 months:** starting from your cash, each month adds your average income and takes
  away your budget (or your average spending where there is no budget). These are estimates,
  labelled **Budget** or **Forecast**. If a month would go below zero, it is highlighted.
