# One-person workspace

Some steps normally need a second person:
- approving a journal entry;
- opening a locked period;
- approving a budget;
- approving a workspace KPI;
- finalising a report pack;
- signing off a VAT return.

If you are the only person in your workspace, turn on **One-person workspace** in **Workspace
settings**. You can then approve your own work. Each such approval is recorded and shown as
**Self-approved (one-person workspace)**, on screen and in exported report packs.

## Turning it on
1. Open **Workspace settings**.
2. Under **One-person workspace**, choose **Turn on**. You may be asked to sign in again first.

It can be turned on only while:
- you are the only member;
- no invitation is pending;
- no accounting firm has an active engagement grant.

If one of these does not hold, the page says which.

## While it is on
- **Journal entries:** submit the entry, then approve it and post it yourself.
- **Locked periods:** request the unlock and approve it. Your one approval opens the period for
  the time you asked for.
- **Budgets, workspace KPIs and report packs:** approve or finalise them yourself.
- **Adding people:** invitations and engagement grants are refused. Turn the setting off first.

## Turning it off
Choose **Turn off**. From then on, a second person must approve again. Earlier approvals stay
marked as self-approved.
