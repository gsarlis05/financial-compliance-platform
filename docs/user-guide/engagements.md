# Firm access (engagements)

A business or a person who uses the platform can let an accounting firm work in their own
workspace. The data stays theirs.

## If you are the client
Open **Setup → Firm access** (workspace owners and administrators).

1. Enter the **firm's address**. The firm gives it to you; it looks like `sarlis-co-1a2b3c`.
2. Choose the **roles**: Accountant (prepares entries), Reviewer (approves and posts) or Read only.
   A personal workspace can grant Read only.
3. Choose the date the access ends, at most 3 years ahead.

The firm's people get at most the roles you chose, and only as far as their own role in the firm
allows. They can never manage your members or your firm access. Everything they do is recorded in
your workspace under their name and firm.

Select **End access** at any time. It takes effect at once: the firm's next click is refused.

## If you are the firm
Open **Setup → Firm access** in the firm workspace (owners, partners and administrators). The page
shows your firm's address to give to clients, and the clients who have let you in.

For each client, **assign** the members of your firm who work on it. An assigned member sees the
client in the workspace list, marked **through your firm**, and works there with the normal
screens. **Remove** a member to end their access.

Clients with an engagement also appear in **Cash & planning → Firm portfolio**, marked **client's
own workspace**.
