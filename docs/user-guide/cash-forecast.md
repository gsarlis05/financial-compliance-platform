# The 13-week cash forecast

**Cash & planning → Cash forecast** shows whether you will have enough cash over the next 13 weeks.

## Reading it
- **Inputs:** the forecast starts from the cash on your bank and cash accounts at the as-of date,
  then adds:
  - open customer invoices, at their due date plus that customer's usual days late
    (**Customers & suppliers → Payment behaviour**);
  - open supplier invoices, by your payment policy;
  - rent, payroll and other obligations, and loan payments;
  - tax payments and manual items (**Obligations, loans & tax**).
- **Nature:** every amount says what it is, and the chart uses line styles, never colour alone:
  - **Actual:** what the books show (the opening cash). It is a solid point on the chart.
  - **Committed:** contractually due. **Expected:** likely but not contractual.
  - **Forecast:** a week's totals, which mix the two. It is a dashed line on the chart.
  - **Hypothetical:** a what-if. It is a dotted line on the chart, and never saved.
  - **Target:** your minimum cash. It is a thin line on the chart.
- **Shortfall:** a week whose closing cash is below the minimum is marked "!" in the chart and in
  the table. The summary gives the first day cash drops below the minimum.
- **Headroom** is closing cash minus the minimum. **Liquidity** is closing cash plus your
  overdraft and credit facility limits.
- **Week detail:** select a week to see its flows. Select a flow to see the items behind it (each
  invoice, scheduled payment or manual item).

## Keeping it current
- **Out of date:** a yellow box says when the forecast is out of date, and why, for example new
  postings or changed open items. **Run it again now** makes a new forecast; the old one is kept
  under **Earlier forecasts**. Forecasts are also refreshed automatically about every 15 minutes
  once that job is switched on where the platform is hosted.
- **Data quality:** warnings include a bank account not reconciled recently and disputed invoices
  left out. Fix them for a more reliable forecast.

## What-if
Add one or more changes and select **Compare with the forecast**. The changes can be:
- a customer, supplier or category paying earlier or later;
- a category or party changing by a percentage;
- a one-off receipt or payment.

The table gains a **What-if change** column, and the chart a dotted line. Nothing is saved: the
forecast itself never changes.

## Accuracy
For each past week, the dashboard shows what was forecast 1, 4 and 13 weeks earlier against what
actually moved on the cash accounts, and the average error. It fills in as forecasts accumulate
week by week.
