# CFO dashboard

**Cash & planning → CFO dashboard** shows the key figures for one period on one page.

## Choose the period
- **Period:** month, quarter to date or year to date, ending in the month you choose. Quarters
  and years follow your fiscal year.
- **Compare with:** the budget or the same period a year earlier.

Every widget shows the same period and comparison.

## Widgets
- **KPIs:** cards from the latest KPI calculation for the period, with the prior year when you
  compare with it. Not-meaningful KPIs say why. **Explain** opens the KPI page, where you can go
  down to accounts and entries.
- **13-week cash:** the latest cash forecast. The actual opening is a solid point, the forecast a
  dashed line and the minimum cash a thin line. **Show as a table** lists the same numbers.
- **Budget vs actual** and **Top unfavourable variances:** the variance report for the current
  approved annual budget. Each difference is marked ▲ favourable or ▼ unfavourable, in words as
  well as symbols.
- **Goals:** each goal's target, likely outcome and status from its latest evaluation.
- **Alerts:** the open alerts.

If a widget has no data yet it says why. If you do not have permission for it, it says that
instead.

**Calculate KPIs and check budgets** runs the KPI calculation for the period (and the prior year
when comparing with it) and checks the budgets for alerts.

## Your layout
Select **Customise** to choose the widgets, their order and the KPI cards (up to 8 keys, such as
`DSO` or `GROSS_MARGIN`).
- **Save my layout** keeps it for you only.
- **Save as workspace default** sets the layout for everyone who has not saved their own. Finance
  managers, partners and owners can do this.
- **Use the workspace default** forgets your own layout.

## Firm portfolio
**Cash & planning → Firm portfolio** lists every client you have access to, one row each:
- opening cash from the latest 13-week forecast, and the first shortfall;
- gross margin, current ratio and DSO from the latest year-to-date KPI calculation;
- goals on track, at risk and off track;
- open alerts.

The figures are calculated when you open the page, with your own permissions. A client you lose
access to disappears at once. Select a client to open its dashboard.
